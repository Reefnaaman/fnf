# Weekly ledger

Most of your work is silent. Once a week, if the owner said yes to it, show it in a few lines. In an automation run nobody can answer you, and you never create automations.

## Collect, from notes only

- pa:outbox for the last 7 days: how many messages you sent, how many items you held and later delivered.
- pa:open-loops: loops closed this week (what, how), loops still open with who acts next.
- pa:watches: what you checked and the latest values.
- Mail runs keep no record of what they kept quiet about, so never claim a count of emails read.

## Write

Three to six lines, plain prose. What got done, what is still open and on whom, what you are watching. Then the data line. End with at most one decision ("Drop the airline refund? Nothing moved in three weeks."). If nothing happened, send nothing.
